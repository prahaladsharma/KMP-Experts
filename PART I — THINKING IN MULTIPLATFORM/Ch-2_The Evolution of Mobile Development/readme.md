```markdown id="ch2-overview"
# 📘 Chapter 2 — The Evolution of Mobile Development

> *Understanding how mobile development evolved to appreciate why Kotlin Multiplatform represents the next step in modern application architecture.*

This chapter explores the journey of mobile development—from the early days of native applications to the emergence of cross-platform frameworks and, ultimately, Kotlin Multiplatform. Rather than comparing technologies, we'll examine the engineering challenges each generation attempted to solve and the valuable lessons they left behind.

---

## 📖 Chapter Overview

### **Part 1 — Native Mobile Era**

Explore the origins of modern mobile development and understand why native applications became the industry standard. We'll look at the strengths of platform-specific development and how growing product complexity gradually exposed its limitations.

---

### **Part 2 — Rise of Cross-Platform Frameworks**

Discover why organizations began searching for alternatives to maintaining separate Android and iOS codebases. This section examines the business and engineering motivations that led to the rise of cross-platform development.

---

### **Part 3 — Flutter, React Native, Xamarin**

Take a closer look at the major cross-platform frameworks that shaped the mobile industry. We'll compare their architectural philosophies, strengths, trade-offs, and the lessons they taught developers about code sharing and native experiences.

---

### **Part 4 — Why Kotlin Multiplatform Is Different**

Learn how Kotlin Multiplatform takes a fundamentally different approach by focusing on sharing business logic instead of the entire application. We'll explore why this architectural philosophy allows teams to reduce duplication while preserving fully native user experiences.

---

### **Part 5 — Future of Mobile Development**

Look ahead at the future of mobile engineering and discover how modern teams are shifting from platform-centric development to product-centric architecture. We'll explore why business logic has become the most valuable asset in software and how Kotlin Multiplatform aligns with the next generation of application development.

---

## 🎯 Learning Outcomes

After completing this chapter, you'll be able to:

- Understand how mobile development has evolved over the past decade.
- Explain why cross-platform frameworks emerged and the problems they aimed to solve.
- Compare the architectural approaches of Flutter, React Native, Xamarin, and Kotlin Multiplatform.
- Recognize why Kotlin Multiplatform focuses on sharing business logic instead of the entire application.
- Appreciate the architectural trends shaping the future of modern mobile development.
```
