```markdown id="ch1-overview"
# 📘 Chapter 1 — Stop Writing the Same Business Logic Twice

> *Understanding the engineering problem before learning the solution.*

This chapter lays the foundation for the entire handbook by exploring why Kotlin Multiplatform was created. Instead of jumping into code, we'll examine the real engineering challenges that arise as mobile applications grow and why sharing business logic has become a key architectural decision for modern development teams.

---

## 📖 Chapter Overview

### **Part 1 — The Real Engineering Problem**

Discover the hidden cost of maintaining separate Android and iOS codebases. We'll explore how duplicated business logic impacts development speed, maintenance, testing, and long-term scalability.

---

### **Part 2 — Business Logic vs Platform Logic**

Learn how to distinguish between platform-specific code and business logic. Understanding this separation is essential for identifying what should be shared across platforms and what should remain native.

---

### **Part 3 — Why JetBrains Built KMP**

Explore the motivation behind Kotlin Multiplatform and the engineering philosophy that guided its design. Rather than replacing native development, KMP was created to eliminate unnecessary duplication while preserving the strengths of each platform.

---

### **Part 4 — Architectural Mindset & Decision Framework**

Conclude the chapter by adopting a product-first architectural mindset. We'll discuss when Kotlin Multiplatform is the right choice, when native development is a better fit, and introduce a practical decision framework for evaluating real-world projects.

---

## 🎯 Learning Outcomes

After completing this chapter, you'll be able to:

- Understand the core problem Kotlin Multiplatform was designed to solve.
- Differentiate between business logic and platform-specific logic.
- Explain the architectural philosophy behind Kotlin Multiplatform.
- Evaluate whether KMP is the right choice for a project using a structured decision-making approach.
```
